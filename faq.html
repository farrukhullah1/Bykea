Partners
How do I become a Bykea Partner? ⌃

Applying to be a Bykea partner is simple. Download the Bykea Partner app or visit the nearest Bykea Franchise to register. For more details visit the Bykea Partner Page.

I want to become a Bykea Partner but don’t own a bike. ⌄

We will connect you with a financial institution that provides easy installment plans or leases. In some areas, a Bykea partner can earn up to 5 times the monthly installment – making owning a bike easy.

Are Bykea Partners insured? ⌄

Safety of our customers and partners is the utmost priority. We provide our partners and customers an option of getting themselves insured during the ride.

Businesses
My parcel got damaged during delivery, now what? ⌃

Our Bykea partners are trained and understand the importance of delivering your items with care. However, we provide an added layer of safety through an opt-in insurance for parcels so even if there is any odd chance that it gets damaged, your valuable items are completely protected.
